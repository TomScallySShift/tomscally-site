Signal Shift grew out of a recurring problem Tom saw across those areas: companies often have substantial internal knowledge and conviction, but lack an independent way to test whether the outside market supports the same assumptions before significant resources are committed.

His role in Signal Shift is the human decision layer — combining outside evidence with commercial judgment to help leadership determine whether to Proceed, Adjust, or Reconsider before the next move.

Signal Shift is the diagnosis — what's supported, what's exposed, what's missing. When a blind spot turns out to be an opportunity, Tom's broader experience in partnerships, business development, and positioning is what helps turn that into a next step, not just an observation.

The system provides the evidence. Experience provides the judgment. Leadership makes the decision.
