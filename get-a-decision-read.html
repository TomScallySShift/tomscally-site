What is Signal Shift?

Signal Shift is an independent, outside-in decision diagnostic designed to help leadership pressure-test an important commercial move before committing significant time, money, credibility, or momentum.

It tests the assumptions behind the decision against current external evidence — and can return after the move to determine whether the market responded as expected.

Why can't I just ask ChatGPT whether my strategy will work?

You can.

AI can analyze the information and assumptions it's given. Signal Shift independently establishes and examines the external evidence against which those assumptions should be tested.

The difference isn't AI versus human. It's independent evidence + a structured methodology + experienced commercial judgment applied to a specific decision.

Does Signal Shift predict whether my decision will succeed?

No — and we won't give you a precise probability we can't defend.

Signal Shift evaluates how well the assumptions behind a decision are supported by current external evidence.
